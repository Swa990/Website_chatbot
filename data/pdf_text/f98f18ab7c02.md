Note:
1) The Schedule of Charges was last updated on 1st March 2022.
2) Above fees/charges are inclusive of 5% Value Added Tax (VAT) in line with the UAE CB and Federal Tax Authority regulations.
3) Instructions received by letter / fax/ Compact Disc (CD) at our Branches / Processing Centers / Relationship Teams are treated as Counter
Transactions.
4) Alternate Channels include electronic & vendor-based channels for Corporates - smartBUSINESS, businessONLINE, smartBUSINESS Connect,
Cash Pick-up and Delivery, Smart Cash Deposit Machines, SWIFT SCORE, Open Banking API etc.
5) Above charges are Emirates NBD charges only, other banks may deduct their charges from the remittance amount or debit it separately to your
account as per actuals. Please click here to view correspondent bank charges.
6) Courier charges and out of pocket expenses will be as per actuals.
7) Bulk transaction handling capabilities are offered through alternate channels for Cash Pick-up and Delivery, Smart Cash Deposit Machines, Cheque
Deposit and Transfers.
8) Cash processing fee may apply depending on the number of notes and coins.
9) The charges are levied per account participating in the Cash Concentration or Notional Pooling structure. Relevant transfer charges will apply per
sweep.
10) Additional charge for physical warehousing of post-dated cheques will be levied.
11) Transactions initiated using smartBUSINESS and businessONLINE Self Service will be charged at Bank Counter rates.
12) Cheque book issuance charges are based on the number of cheque books issued and not on the number of leaves within the cheque book.
13) Emirates NBD reserves the right to charge Handling commission for documents released free of payment against payment / Co-acceptance /
Avalization fee / Payment commission / Transfer bill charges / Amendment instruction.
14) N/A indicates that the service is not applicable on the channel.
15) Correspondent Guarantee Issuing Bank’s charges are charged as per actuals.
16) Cheques issued by ENBD’s client.
