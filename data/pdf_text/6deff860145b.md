Key Facts Statement (KFS)
Emirates NBD Pay
This KFS provides you with key product features and indicative information about the fees and charges applicable to
Emirates NBD Pay merchant acquiring services.

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Point of Sale (POS) | Accept in-person card payments using an Emirates NBD Pay payment terminal. |
| Pay by Link (PBL) | Generate and share payment links with customers to collect card payments remotely. |
| Payment Gateway (PG) | Accept payments through your approved website or online sales channels. |
| Emirates NBD Pay Dashboard | Access transaction data, trends and insights, initiate eligible voids and refunds and generate Pay by Link payment links through the Emirates NBD Pay Portal. |

| Item | Fee / key information |
| --- | --- |
| Merchant Service Fee (MSF) | As agreed in your merchant application / pricing offer. |
| Transaction Fee | As agreed in your merchant application / pricing offer. |
| One-time / recurring service fees | As agreed in your merchant application / pricing offer. |
| Refund Processing Fee | AED 5 per refund. |
| Chargeback Processing Fee | AED 5 per chargeback. |
| Settlement Frequency | T+1, unless otherwise agreed in the merchant application or subsequently notified by the Bank. |
| Early Termination Fee – Monthly Plan | AED 800 one-time fee where a merchant subscribing on a monthly payment basis terminates the agreement within six months from the commencement date. |
| Equipment Loss / Damage | Repair or replacement cost, up to the full value of the equipment, may be charged where equipment is lost or damaged, excluding fair wear and tear. |
| Other Fees, Fines and Levies | As applicable under the merchant acquiring terms and conditions and applicable card scheme rules. |

Additional information
• Emirates NBD Pay is subject to the merchant application and merchant acquiring terms and conditions, together with
applicable card scheme rules and applicable laws.
• Settlement proceeds may be adjusted for applicable Refunds, Chargebacks, Reversals, VAT, Fines and Levies, Fees
and Charges and other amounts payable under the merchant acquiring terms and conditions.
• Refunds and Chargebacks are subject to applicable card scheme rules and the merchant acquiring terms and
conditions. Refunds must not exceed the original transaction value and must be returned to the same card used for the
original transaction.
• Where payment equipment is provided, you are responsible for keeping it secure and in good working condition.
Repair or replacement costs may be recovered for loss or damage other than fair wear and tear.
• You must comply with applicable PCI DSS, card scheme and data-security requirements and immediately notify the
Bank if you become aware of or suspect a compromise of cardholder or payment transaction data.
• Information provided to the Bank must remain true, complete and accurate. You must notify the Bank of relevant
changes to your business, ownership or approved business activities.
• Additional or add-on services requested from time to time may be subject to separate terms, conditions and
applicable charges.
• The Bank may amend the merchant acquiring terms and conditions and applicable Fees and Charges by providing
notice in accordance with the agreement and applicable law.
• For enquiries, assistance or complaints, please contact Emirates NBD through the available Business Banking service
channels or visit: https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/help-and-support/contact-us
