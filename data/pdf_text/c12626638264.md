Key Facts Statement (KFS)
Auto Loan – Fixed & Variable Interest Rate
A flexible auto loan to own the car of your dreams. This product is an installment loan. This KFS provides you with key product
features and indicative information about interest, fees and charges. Kindly refer to our Welcome Letter for the final terms of
your loan.
Product Information - Auto Loans
Loan Type Auto Loan
Loan Amount Up to AED 2 Million for individuals and up to AED 7 Million for entity (up to
80% of vehicle cost including accessories for individuals and up to 90%
of vehicle cost including accessories for entity)
Down Payment Minimum 20% of the total vehicle cost for individuals and Minimum 10% of
the total vehicle cost for entity
Loan Currency AED (Arab Emirates Dirham)
Loan Period Min. 12 months (1 year) and Max. 60 months (5 years)
Interest Rate (indicative) Individuals: Interest rate starting from 3.75% up to 9.00% on reducing
balance per annum and remain unchanged for loan tenor
Entity: Interest rate starting from 5.65% up to 10.00% on reducing balance
per annum and remain unchanged for loan tenor
Annualised Percentage Starting from 4.38% up to 12.00%. APR is a reference rate, which includes
Rate (APR) (indicative) applicable interest, fees and charges of the product, expressed as an
annualized rate.
Loan Repayment Your loan repayment is on Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) basis and is
fixed for the entire loan period for fixed rate and may vary based on
EIBOR for variable rate. EMI is calculated by adding the principal loan
amount and total interest and dividing this total by the loan period in
months. This is done using a reducing balance method which means
that as your loan balance reduces, with every Equated Monthly
Instalment (EMI) payment, the interest portion of your EMI will reduce.
1 When variable interest rate is linked to EIBOR (The Emirates Interbank Offered Rate) i.e., EIBOR + Fixed Margin, the EMI will change depending
on the actual EIBOR rate on the review date. You can choose variable price linked to 1 months EIBOR. Variable pricing linked to 1−month EIBOR
will be reviewed monthly and variable pricing linked to 6−months EIBOR will be reviewed every 6 months.
Key Fees & Charges

VAT for individuals
1.05% of the loan amount (Min AED 525) inclusive of VAT for Entity
This is a non-refundable fee, and will not be refunded in case you decide
to withdraw the loan application
Early Settlement / 1.05% of outstanding principal amount (inclusive of VAT)
Foreclosure /
Late Payment Fee AED 52.5 (inclusive of VAT) is billed only when the monthly repayment is
not received in full by the due date.
