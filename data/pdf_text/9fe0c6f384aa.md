Key Facts Statement (KFS)
Semi Secured Business Loan
(with property as collateral)
Fixed Interest Rate for SME
Enjoy liquidity with Semi Secured Business Loan to grow your business, meet your working capital requirements or any other
Business need without liquidating your property. This KFS provides you with key product features and indicative information
about interest, fees, and charges. Kindly refer to our sanction letter for the final terms of your loan.
Product Information
Loan Type Business Loan with collateral on existing property extended to customer
looking for Entities expansion
Loan Currency AED (Arab Emirates Dirham)
Loan Amount Up to AED 5,000,000
Loan Period Min. 12 months (1 year) and Max. 60 months (5 years)
Interest Rate (indicative) Starting from 7.5% up to 10.5% per annum, calculated on reducing balance.
Fixed for the duration of the loan
Annualised Percentage Starting from 7.61% up to 10.61% per annum. APR is a reference rate, which
Rate (APR) (Indicative) includes applicable interest, fees, and charges of the product, expressed as
an annualised rate.
Loan Repayment Loan is repaid through Equated Monthly Installment (EMI), fixed for the entire
loan period. EMI is calculated by adding the principal loan amount and total
interest and dividing this total by the loan period in months.
Key Fees & Charges
Loan Processing Fee 1.05% of the loan amount, inclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT)
This is a non-refundable fee, and will not be returned, in case you decide to
withdraw the loan application
Early Settlement/ 5.25% of outstanding principal amount (inclusive of VAT)
Foreclosure Fee
Partial Settlement Fee 5.25% of payment amount (inclusive of VAT)
Late Payment Fee AED 420 (inclusive of VAT), if the monthly repayment is not received in full,
by the due date
Valuation Charges AED 3,150 onetime charge if customer choose to evaluate the property with us
Copies of Property AED 105 per instance per document requested
Related Documents
Property Insurance fees Property Insurance Fee of 0.05% per annum
Property Insurance premium is calculated on the property value or loan amount
whichever is higher at the time of loan approval
Important Links
For full and latest fees & charges and other product details please visit our website
www.emiratesnbd.com/kfs or scan QR code
