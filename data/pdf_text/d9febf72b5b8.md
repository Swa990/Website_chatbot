The Home Insurance policy protects you against the costs associated with any unexpected event that could
arise while you are living in your own home as the owner/while renting out you’re building to someone
else/while living you’re in your home as a tenant.
Eligibility of cover:
Depending on the type of occupancy:
• Owner living in: Building, Contents (including domestic staff), Personal Belongings section.
• Owner renting out: Building section only.
• Tenant: Contents (including domestic staff), Personal Belongings section.
Age Criteria: Must be 18 years and older at the time of inception of policy.
Documentation required:
• Passport or Emirates ID – Mandatory.
• Title deed for building owners or bank - As requested.
• Ejari or rental agreement for tenants - As requested.
Single Article Limit:
• Contents: Any single item valued above AED 40,000 must be specified on the policy schedule.
• Personal possessions: Any single item valued above AED 10,000 must be specified on the policy schedule.
Excess:
• Minimum excess applicable for Contents is AED 250.
• Minimum excess applicable for Portable Equipment (Mobile phones, PED's, Eye accessories) is AED 500.
• An additional excess of 10% of the claim amount applies for specified items above the single article limit.
• Minimum excess applicable for Personal Possessions is AED 250.
Key Exclusions:
• Loss or damage not reported to the Police.
• Loss or damage arising due to any deliberate acts by you or your household or your guests, employees, or
tenants, depending on the type of cover offered.
• Loss or damage occurring after your home has been unoccupied for 60 consecutive days.
• Loss or damage occurring before cover commences.
• Loss or damage occurring due to wear and tear any gradually operating clause.
• Loss or damage caused by any faulty workmanship, defective design, or the use of defective materials.
• Consequential loss of any kind or description by you or your household.
For further clarity, please read and refer to your policy terms and conditions.
Claims:
You may contact the claims team on homeclaims.ae@livainsurance.com.com if you wish to make a claim.
Alternatively, you may contact us at the following numbers:
Tel: 04 3029835 ,04 3029903
Please Note:
When submitting a claim, you will be required to provide a valuation or proof of purchase for any single article of
Content above AED 20,000 or single article of Personal possession over AED 5,000.

Cancellations:
You may cancel the policy by giving us at least seven days written notice and you will get a pro-rata refund of the
premium for any unexpired period of cover.
Your obligations:
• You must answer our questions honestly, accurately and provide true and complete information, and tell us of
any changes in your circumstances that may affect your insurance and the cover provided.
• You must tell us as soon as you become aware of any incident or legal proceeding which may lead to a claim.
• You must comply with all the conditions set out in the policy.
• You must pay the premium shown on the policy schedule.
Please refer to the Home Insurance Policy Terms & Conditions document for definitions, descriptions, and
exclusions related to the policy.
Your period of insurance is shown on your policy schedule. These form the term of your policy.
If you would like to change your level of cover or have any other queries, please call our dedicated Customer Service
Centre on 800 – 772.
