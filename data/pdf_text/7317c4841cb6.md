Correspondent Bank charges for
International Transfers
Correspondent Bank charges are applicable when charge code “OUR” is selected and Telegraphic
Transfers are routed via Emirates NBD direct correspondents. Additional charges may apply if an
intermediary bank is involved.
For charge code BEN/SHA, beneficiary bank(s) and correspondent bank(s) charges may be deducted
from the transaction amount.
Note:
For AED transfers outside the UAE, a fee of AED 105 is paid as correspondent bank charges to the
Central Bank of the UAE.
For AED transfers within UAE, a fee of AED 1.05 is paid as correspondent bank charges to the Central
Bank of the UAE.
