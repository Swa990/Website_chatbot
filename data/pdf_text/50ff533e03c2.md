Promotion/Offer Period:
The offer is valid from 1 October to 31 December
2026 (both dates inclusive).
The Offer:
An Eligible account holder who holds a Plus Saver
(AED) Account with the Bank, or opens one during
the Offer Period, may earn bonus interest of up to
2.75% per annum in addition to the applicable
standard interest, based on Table 1 ("Bonus
Interest"), on each of the following qualifying
balances:
(a) the full eligible Net New Money acquired
during the period between 1 April 2026 and 30
June 2026 (both dates inclusive) ("First Campaign
NNM"), provided it was maintained in full
throughout 1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026
(both dates inclusive) and remains maintained in
full throughout the Offer Period;
(b) the full eligible Net New Money acquired
during the period between 1 July 2026 and 30

September 2026 (both dates inclusive) ("Second
Campaign NNM"), provided it remains maintained
in full throughout the Offer Period; and
(c) eligible Net New Money deposited and
maintained during the Offer Period ("Current
Campaign NNM"). Each qualifying balance will be
assessed separately. Failure to satisfy the
conditions for one balance will not affect another
balance that independently satisfies these
Promotion Terms and Conditions.
Net New Money (NNM) refers to funds new to
Emirates NBD Group (Emirates NBD Bank,
Emirates Islamic Bank & Liv Bank) ,deposited in
your Plus Saver (AED) account during the Offer
period (as specified in these terms and
conditions) resulting in average balance increase
in the Plus Saver (AED) account as well as an
increase in the customer’s total relationship
balance for their CIF with the Bank during the
Offer period. Funds originating from within the
Emirates NBD Group, including Emirates Islamic
Bank and Liv Bank, will not be considered NNM.
For the Offer Period, Current Campaign NNM will
be the lower of:
(i) the net increase in the average Plus Saver
Account balance compared with the
closing balance on 30 September 2026;
and
(ii) the net increase in the Eligible Customer’s
average total relationship balance at CIF
level during the Offer Period.
Eligible Account holder refers to an individual
who is an existing customer holding a Plus Saver
Account with the Bank or a New-to-Bank
customer who opens a new Plus Saver Account
during the Offer Period.
