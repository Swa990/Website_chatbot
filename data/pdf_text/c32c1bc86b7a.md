Important upcoming changes to Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange (ADX) > After 17th January 2025, the order type ‘Good till session’ will no longer be available for ADX
listed securities.
The exchange symbols for the securities mentioned in the table below, are being updated as follows:
> Please note that the symbol changes by ADX will automatically be reflected on ENBD X
allowing no interruption to the usual trading services from 20th January 2025 onwards.
> These symbol changes will have no impact on your existing holdings.
> Due to the above changes, outstanding pending orders on any ADX listed securities will be
cancelled automatically by ADX after close of business on 17th January 2025. Given this impact
to outstanding pending orders, you will be able to place new orders, if required, with the new
security symbols on and after 20th January 2025.
