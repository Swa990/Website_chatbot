FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
EduCare
Key Benefits
1. Who is underwriting the risk of this insurance policy?
The EduCare is a unique insurance product offered by Liva Insurance exclusively for Emirates NBD BANK customers.
2. Am I eligible?
Eligibility Criteria
The Insured Person must be an eligible cardholder who is granted Credit Card Facility by the Policyholder and meet the following
criteria at the time of enrollment into this Policy:
• Meet the eligibility conditions stipulated by the Bank/Policyholder.
• Be a UAE citizen/ resident.
• Be gainfully employed or self-employed for gain or reward.
• Be older than 18 years and under 64 years, at the commencement date. For better clarity, please note the following age limits:
Minimum age at entry : 18 years
Maximum age at entry : 64 years
Maximum age at claim : 65 years
3. Can supplementary Emirates NBD BANK credit card holders enrol for this product?
No
4. Currency of the policy
This insurance policy is in UAE Dirhams (AED).
5. Is claims payment to a third party allowed?
All insurance claim amount under this policy will be paid only to the Insured Person’s legal heirs.
6. What is the validity period of this policy?
This is a monthly pay, monthly cover product. Following the expiry of the period of the monthly coverage as stated in the Certificate
of Insurance, this Policy will be auto renewed every month from term to term by payment of premium. The coverage under this

7. Free Look period
This insurance policy offers you a free-look period for 30 days from the commencement date (new enrolments only) and upon
cancellation within the free-look period, you will be entitled to a full refund subject to no claims.
8. Is this policy portable to other countries?
No, this Insurance policy is for UAE residents/Citizens.
9. Who can be nominated as Beneficiary?
The beneficiaries under this policy are the Insured Person’s legal heirs.
10. What are the things that I will not be covered for?
The policy wording lists down the exclusions applicable to each section, please refer to the EduCare policy wording document.
11. Can I change the Beneficiary during the policy period?
No, the beneficiaries under this policy are the Insured Person’s legal heirs.
12. How do I claim?
Upon happening of an event giving rise to a claim the Company needs to be notified of such event within the stipulated time
frame and submit the necessary documents as requested by the Company. You may contact the Company by phone on +971 4
302 9835/9903 and by email at nmclaims.ae@livainsurance.com. For detailed information and process, please refer to the Claims
Procedure section in the EduCare policy wording.
