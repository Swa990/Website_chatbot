Understanding Risks
Upon investing in a Mutual Fund, a prospectus detailing the risks involved in Mutual
Funds will be given to you. It is imperative that you go through this document
completely, so that you are aware of all the general and specific risks involved.
Finding a Mutual Fund that works for you
The selection of Mutual Funds would depend on your investment profile, i.e. appetite
for risk and your investment goals. You do not have to guess your profile.
There are sophisticated tools available in the market that generate your profile
based on your responses to a set of questions.
Our proprietary tool helps us understand the type of investor you are, either cautious,
moderate or adventurous and suggests a right solution for you.
