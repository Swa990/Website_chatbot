Frequently Asked Questions
Can I setup a Direct Debit Service to
transfer funds from one account to
another within the UAE?
Yes, Direct Debit Service is available across all banks
in the UAE. However, you must register as an
Originator with the Central Bank through
Emirates NBD.
Can I amend/cancel a Direct Debit
mandate setup?
Yes, you can amend/cancel the registered mandate
through Emirates NBD and ensure the payer
mandate is provided as supporting document.
