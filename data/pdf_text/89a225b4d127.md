Emirates NBD Millionaire Promotion Terms and
Conditions (‘Promotion Terms and Conditions’)
Emirates NBD Bank (P.J.S.C.) (the ‘Bank’ or ‘Emirates NBD’) will be rewarding eligible customers with the
opportunity of entering into a prize draw to win monthly, quarterly as well as annual grand prizes (hereafter
referred to as the ‘Promotion’).
The Promotion is subject to: (i) the Bank’s General Terms and Conditions for Consumer Banking Products
and the Bank’s General Terms and Conditions for Business Banking Products, as may be amended from time
to time (the ‘ENBD GTCs’) available on the Bank’s website at https://www.emiratesnbd.com/tncs (ii) Liv
General Terms and Conditions (‘Liv GTCs’), available at
https://cdn.emiratesnbd.com/liv/pdf/Liv_Terms_conditions_en.pdf and (iii) these Promotion Terms and
Conditions. In the event of any conflict between these Promotion Terms and Conditions, the ENBD GTCs and
Liv GTCs, these Promotion Terms and Conditions shall prevail, but only in relation to this Promotion. Terms used
in title case and not defined herein shall have the same meaning as given to them in the ENBD GTCs or Liv GTCs,
as applicable.
1. Promotion Period:
The ‘Promotion Period’ shall be valid from 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2027 (both dates inclusive).
2. Eligible Account:
The Promotion is applicable to eligible customers holding one or more of the following accounts during
the Promotion Period (each an ‘Eligible Account’):
a) Millionaire Current Account
b) Millionaire Savings Account
c) Emirati Millionaire Savings Account
d) Minor Account
e) Millionaire Account (Liv)
f) All Business Banking Packages
3. Reward Categories:
The Promotion will have the following three (3) reward categories:
a) Annual Grand Draw One Annual Grand Draw will be conducted, with one winner selected across Pool 1,
Pool 2 or Pool 3 to receive a cash prize at the end of the Promotion Period (‘Annual Grand Draw’)
b) Quarterly Draws for Pool 1, Pool 2 and Pool 3 (‘Quarterly Draws’)
c) Monthly Draws for customers in Pool 1, Pool 2, Pool 3 and Pool 4 (‘Monthly Draws’)
4. Customers in Personal Banking, Priority Banking, Business Banking, Private Banking and Liv will be
eligible to participate in the Promotion.
5. All existing individual customers (i.e., Personal Banking, Priority Banking or Private Banking and Liv) will
be automatically registered for this Promotion and be eligible for these prize draws. New-to-bank
individual customers shall be enrolled once they open any eligible Millionaire account.

6. Non-individual customers both existing and new-to-bank under all Business Banking packages and
Business Banking VIP Privilege program must opt in to be eligible for this promotion. Additionally,
non-individual customers under Priority, Personal or Private Banking must also opt in to be eligible for
the promotion.
7. Classification of customers and balances will be considered as follows:
a) ‘Existing’, if banking relationship with Eligible Account(s) and package(s) are opened on or before
the campaign period 31 August 2026, and
b) ‘New’, if banking relationship with Eligible Account(s) and package(s) are opened on or after 1
September 2026 until the end of the Promotion Period.
c) ‘New-to-bank funds’ means net new funds credited into the customer’s account provided there
is an increase in the customer’s Total Relationship Balance. Funds originating from Emirates Islamic
and Liv will not be considered as New-to-bank funds.
8. The Promotion will have four (4) reward pools, each a ‘Pool’
a) Pool 1: Individual UAE National customers and eligible UAE National entities (Emirati)
b) Pool 2: Individual expat customers (non-Emirati)
c) Pool 3: Non-individual expat entities holding a Business Banking Account (non-Emirati)
d) Pool 4: Minor customers holding a Minor Account
9. Promotion Eligibility Criteria and Prize structure - Total Millionaire Winners Across Pool 1, Pool 2, Pool
3 and Pool 4: 509 and Total Prize Value: AED 35.6 million
a) One (1) Annual Grand Draw winner: one (1) winner from across Pool 1, Pool 2 and Pool 3 who will
receive AED 10,000,000.
b) Sixteen (16) Quarterly Draw winners: four (4) winners from Pool 1, eight (8) winners from Pool 2 and
four (4) winners from Pool 3, with each winner receiving AED 1,000,000.
c) Thirty-six (36) Monthly Draw winners: one (1) winner from each of Pool 1, Pool 2 and Pool 3 per
month, with each winner receiving AED 100,000.
d) Thirty-six (36) Monthly Draw winners: one (1) winner from each of Pool 1, Pool 2 and Pool 3 per
month, with each winner receiving AED 50,000.
e) Three hundred and sixty (360) Monthly Draw winners: ten (10) winners from each of Pool 1, Pool 2
and Pool 3 per month, with each winner receiving AED 10,000.
f) Sixty (60) Minor Monthly Draw winners from Pool 4: five (5) winners per month, with each winner
receiving AED 10,000.
10. Eligibility conditions for the Promotion are set out below:
a) The above-mentioned Pools (except Pool 4) will have entries in the Annual Grand Draw from
Existing, New-to-bank (‘NTB’) and New-to-liabilities (‘NTL’) customers who have increased the
average balance across all Eligible Account(s), and/or opened an Eligible Account during the
Promotion Period.
b) A minimum monthly average balance (as provided in the table below) during the Promotion Period,
across all Eligible Account(s) is the base requirement to qualify for entry in the Annual Grand Draw, and
c) An additional entry will be given to each customer based on balance increase as per each Pool’s
*minimum average balance above previous month’s average balance amount.

14. General - Further eligibility criteria
a) All references to an ‘increase’ in the monthly balance or the balance over the Promotion Period of
Eligible Account(s) is to an increase in such balances by crediting to the Eligible Account(s) NTB
funds, being funds that are new to the Bank, its subsidiaries, and affiliates (the ‘Bank Group’).
b) Balance amounts and increases in balance amounts in Eligible Accounts shall be assessed by the
Bank with reference to the aggregate of all Eligible Accounts of a single customer and which are
under that customer’s customer information file (‘CIF’).
c) All new customers above shall automatically be registered to participate in this Promotion.
d) All Eligible Account(s) must be valid, active, not suspended, not frozen and must be in full
compliance with ENBD GTCs and Liv GTCs, as applicable, along with these Promotion Terms and
Conditions during the Promotion Period and any time thereafter connected to this Promotion.
e) In the case of joint accounts, only the primary account holder will be eligible to win a prize.
f) Any prize won by a non-individual customer will be awarded to the applicable business entity.
g) The following individuals/entities shall not be eligible to participate in the Promotion:
(i) Permanent and/or contract employees of the Bank Group and their immediate family
members.
(ii) Representatives and/or agents (including advertising and promotion agents) of the Bank
Group and their immediate family members.
h) There is no cap on the maximum number of entries for any of the Monthly, Quarterly or Annual
Grand Draws.

to select the winners.
