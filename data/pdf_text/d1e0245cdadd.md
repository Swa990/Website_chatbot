Wages Protection System (WPS)
Developed and regulated by the Central Bank of UAE,
the electronic salary transfer system covers all
institutions registered with the UAE Ministry of
Labour. Payments can be made through the Salary
Information File (SIF) format as mandated by the
Central Bank in AED, within the UAE only.
One-time registration of the Employer’s
Ministry of Labour ID
One-time implementation of SIF file format
Guaranteed and timely disbursement of
salaries
Get reports for tracking and successful
salary credit response

Non-Wages Protection System
(Non-WPS) Payments
The Non-Wages Protection System (Non-WPS)
supports automated salary payments for companies
that do not fall under the Ministry of Labour. This
service can be accessed through smartBUSINESS -
our online channel for corporates. MIS reports can be
made available to users based on role and authority
matrix.
Process payments in local and foreign
currencies in a Single File Format (SIF)
Allows single debit and multiple credits to
several beneficiaries
Access detailed reports based on role and
authority of corporate users
Reduce times spent on preparing and
uploading multiple salary files
