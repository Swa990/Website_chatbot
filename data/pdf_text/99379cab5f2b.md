Frequently Asked Questions
How can I request for a cheque book and
where will it be delivered?
Cheque books must be requested through
smartBUSINESS - our online business channel for
corporates. The service can be found under the
Cheque Printing product with "Requests" menu.
Cheque books will be delivered to the address
registered with your bank.
Will I be able to customise the cheque
book?
The cheque advice template can be customised with
your company logo. This is achived through a
one-time set up on smartBUSINESS - our online
business channel for corporates.
Would the cheque need to be physically
signed by the authorizer of the company?
Signature of the authoriser is uploaded on the
remote cheque printing application as part of the
on-boarding process. These signatures will be
digitally printed on cheques, based on a pre-defined
online approval workflow.
