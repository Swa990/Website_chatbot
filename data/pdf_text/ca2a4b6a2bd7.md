Personal Motor insurance mainly caters to the insurance needs of the individual customers. Based on the cover
selected Its primary objective is to provide financial protection against physical damage to the insured vehicle and
Third-Party vehicle or bodily injury that could arise from incidents in a vehicle accident.
Eligibility of cover:
The insured must be a UAE resident/ citizen or GCC citizen living/working/based in UAE holding valid UAE driving
license and driving the vehicle registered in UAE.
Documentation required:
• Emirates ID of insured
• UAE driving license.
• Vehicle registration card/ invoice copy
Key Exclusions:
• Loss or damage which occurs to the Motor Vehicle with respect to accident resulting from:
- Use of Motor Vehicle for the purposes other personal domestic commute.
- Violation of laws if violation implies an intentional felony or misdemeanor according to the definition
mentioned in the applicable Federal Penal code.
• If it is proven that the cause of accident is due to Motor Vehicle been used or utilized in a speed race or test.
• Loss and damage that occurs to the Motor vehicle outside the geographical territory mentioned in the policy.
• Any claim arising from you being under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
• Indirect losses that occur to the insured or insured vehicle due to failure of mechanical or electric devices.
• Loss or damage that occurs to the motor vehicle off-road as defined in the policy unless specified in the policy.
• Non GCC, Imported or modified vehicles unless declared in the policy.

transfer.

policy.
