TERMS AND CONDITIONS FOR REDEMPTION OF
PRECIOUS METAL – GOLD
These Terms and Conditions for redemption of Precious Metal – Gold holdings (“Redemption Terms”) govern
any request by a customer (“Applicant”or “you”) to Emirates NBD Bank (P.J.S.C.) (the “Bank”) for redemption/
sale of their investment in Precious Metal – Gold (“Gold”) product offered by the Bank (“Redemption
Request”).
Any Capitalized terms used, but not defined herein, shall have the meanings ascribed to such terms in the
General Terms and in the Terms and Conditions for Precious Metal – Gold accepted by you at the time of your
investment in Gold.
By submitting a Redemption Request to the Bank, you hereby acknowledge and confirm as follows:
• All Redemption Requests for Gold shall be subject to the terms of the Gold T&Cs, including the Terms and
Conditions for Precious Metal – Gold, as may be amended by the Bank from time to time and you agree
to read, understand and be bound by these Redemption Terms together with the Gold T&Cs in relation to
your Redemption Requests.
• You agree to pay all applicable fees and charges levied by the Bank in relation to your redemption
request, including Custody and Insurance Fee, Redemption Charges and Collection Charges or Delivery
Charges ( as applicable) as per the terms of the Summary of Fees and Charges (Annexure 1 to Terms and
Conditions for Precious Metal – Gold).
• Delivery Charges are non-refundable in case of delivery failure (as per these Terms and Conditions) owing
to reasons attributable to you, including where your address registered with the Bank is incorrect.
• You have placed this Redemption Request solely at your discretion, after having duly considered all
circumstances that may influence your decision in this regard, without any recommendation or advice
by the Bank. You agree to bear the full responsibility once the Redeemed Gold Bar is received from any
of the Banks’ assigned agents in case of Physical Redemption Instruction or on receipt of proceeds of
sale of the Gold Bar in UAE Dirhams in your Account as per the Gold T&Cs according to the gold price
determined by the Bank, in case of Cash Redemption Request. You agree not to hold the Bank liable for
any loss, costs or damage that you may sustain owing to your Redemption Request.
Emirates NBD Bank (P.J.S.C.) is regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE.
