Promotion period: The offer is valid from 1 September 2026 to 28 February 2027

Eligibility cards and accounts
Eligible cards:
All Emirates NBD Etihad Guest Visa Elevate Credit Cards issued in the United Arab
Emirates (UAE) (“Eligible Card”).
Eligible accounts:
Standard Current Accounts and Standard Savings Accounts opened with the
Bank (each an "Account").
Eligible customer:
This Promotion is open only to existing Eligible Cardholders or customers who
apply for an Eligible Card, successfully complete onboarding for the Eligible Card
prior to the end of the Promotion Period and fulfil the following requirements:
A) They are Abu Dhabi residents (as per the place of issuance of residency
visa); and
B) They are new-to-Bank or new-to-accounts customers who open their
first ever account with the Bank for the purpose of savings or salary
transfer (savings and salary variants respectively) during the Promotion
Period can enrol in this Promotion.

Promotion details:
Eligible Customers could earn Rewards of up to 1 million Etihad Guest Miles as set
out below.
Card linked terms:
Etihad Guest Miles rewarded to Eligible Cardholders (offered regularly without
enroling in this Promotion):
o 40,000 Etihad Guest Miles within 60 days of payment of joining fee
PLUS
o 60,000 Etihad Guest Miles of spending minimum AED 90,000 on
Eligible Spends within 6 months from card issuance date; OR
o 160,000 Etihad Guest Miles of spending minimum AED 120,000 on
Eligible Spends within 6 months from card issuance date.
o Additional Etihad Guest Miles (offered under this Promotion) upon
spending minimum USD 5,000 on Etihad Airlines within the first 12
billing statements to be awarded post 12 months from card issuance
(applicable only as a combination with a Salary Transfer or Savings
Account under this Promotion):
▪ For UAE Nationals: 200,000 Etihad Guest Miles.
▪ For Expats: 100,000 Etihad Guest Miles.
This Promotion is applicable to active primary Eligible Cardholders only. Eligible
Spends made on supplementary Credit Cards will also count for this Promotion
and will be aggregated with spends by the primary Eligible Cardholder; however,
opt-in and offering of Rewards will only be at the primary Cardholder level.
Eligible Spend means purchase of goods and services minus returns and other
credits. Eligible Spends do not include payment of fees or interest charges, cash
advances, purchases of traveller’s checks, person-to-person payments, the
purchase or reloading of prepaid cards, and purchases of other cash equivalents.

Account linked terms:
Eligible Customers are eligible to earn Etihad Guest Miles as per the following
Account linked terms set out below.
Terms for the salary variant offer:
Minimum salary to be AED 30,000 to participate.
The first salary should be credited within 60 days of the opening of the Salary
Account. Minimum 3 salaries to be credited in the salary account to be eligible to
receive Etihad Guest Miles reward under the salary variant offer.
Minimum balance (as provided below) to be maintained in the Account where the
salary gets transferred.
Eligible Customers will receive the Reward after at least 3 salaries are credited
and subject to meeting the salary and balance requirements stated in these
Promotion Terms and Conditions.
Salary Account customers are eligible to earn additional Etihad Guest Miles basis
balance tiers as set out below.

Minimum balance to be AED 250,000 to be maintained over 6 calendar months in
any of the eligible Accounts to participate.

months of account opening, and 50% of eligible Etihad Guest Miles basis the
average balance during the next 3 calendar months of account opening.
