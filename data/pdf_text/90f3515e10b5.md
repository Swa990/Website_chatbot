SCHEDULE OF CHARGES
PRIORITY BANKING
Criteria for Priority Banking customers:
Your total deposits and/or investments are greater than AED 500,000 OR
Your monthly salary of AED 50,000 or greater is transferred to your Emirates NBD account OR
You have availed a Home Loan of AED 3 million or more from Emirates NBD.
*Applicable rate is the standard Fixed Deposit interest rate on the date the deposit was booked, for the period the deposit amount was maintained.
**Transfers to own Smart S@ver accounts, utility bill payments through Online Banking or Bank induced charges will not be considered as withdrawals. In case the
number of monthly withdrawals are more than the above permissible limits, the interest applied for the month will be at 0.25% p.a. Contd.
