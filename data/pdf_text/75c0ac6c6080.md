Valet Parking

service at multiple UAE locations including Dubai Mall, Dubai Int’l Airport, Yas Mall Abu Dhabi. The VIP valet
parking service can be availed for free only twice in a month at these locations. Your Signature Debit Card
will be charged by the 3rd usage of valet service based on location, service and date of service.
