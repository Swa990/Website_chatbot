HOW TO BUILD A STRONG CV?
TIP SHEET
GENERAL TIPS
• Make sure the layout is clean, clear and simple
• Length: maximum two pages
• Format: use a basic font that is easy to read (e.g. Arial, Calibri)
• Font size: 10 – 11
• Always proof-read to avoid any spelling or grammar errors
• Tailor your CV to the role
• Do not add social media profiles like Instagram or twitter unless the it is required
PERSONAL INFORMATION EDUCATION
• Always add a valid email address • Start with the most recent degree/
• Add a local phone number diploma/certificate
• Professional photo (Optional) – No selfie • Always mention the name of the school/
• Nationality college/university
• Include the degree/diploma, graduation
year and location
OBJECTIVE • Highlight relevant honors, achievements
• Length: short paragraph, maximum or awards you earned
100 words
• Be concise and clear
• Write your key achievements and SKILLS
role responsibilities • Include language proficiency
• Mention your skills and personal qualities • Include professional and personal skills
that make you suitable for the role • Length: maximum 10 skills
• Mention your career goals • Up to 5 skills
WORK EXPERIENCE GENERAL INFORMATION
• Start with the most recent role • Add your hobbies – up to 4
• Always mention job title, duration
and location
• Highlight awards and achievements, if any
