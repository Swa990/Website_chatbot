Valet Parking
With your Emirates NBD Visa Infinite Debit Card, you can enjoy Premium Valet parking service at multiple
UAE locations including Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates & Dubai Int’l Airport. The VIP valet parking service
can be availed for free only twice in a month at these locations. Your Infinite Debit Card will be *charged
by the 3rd usage of valet service based on location, service, and date of service.
Here is how it works at Mall of the Emirates:
• The offer is valid March 1st , 2023, to February 28th , 2025.
• It is understood that the Valet Parking Service is subject to availability and that parking spaces
might be sold out at certain times.
• Vehicles are accepted by TVC parking management (valet company) for parking only.
• If you have exceeded 2 free parking service in a month, you will be charged AED 175 for VIP
parking and AED 85 for Standard valet parking on your Visa infinite Debit Card
• TVC parking management will follow a validation process by checking the Visa infinite Debit Card
physically and by inserting your Visa Infinite Debit card into its payment terminal and charge the
card AED 1. Such 1 AED will be refunded to the cardholder immediately by the TVC parking
management after the validation.
• Valet company (TVC parking management) will assume no liability for fire, theft, or damage. In no
event will TVC parking management assume liability for damage or injury sustained through faulty
brakes or other equipment failure.
