Key Facts Statement (KFS)
Loan Against Fixed Deposits for Entities - Fixed Rate
Enjoy liquidity up to 90% of your fixed deposit value with our loan against fixed deposit, without liquidating your fixed deposit.
This KFS provides you with key product features and indicative information about interest, fees, and charges. Kindly refer to our
Welcome Letter for the final terms of your loan.
Product Information
Loan Type Cash Loan with lien on Fixed Deposit/s
Loan Currency AED (Arab Emirates Dirham)
Loan Amount Up to AED 20,000,000
Loan Period Min. 12 months (1 year) and Max. 48 months (4 years)
Interest Rate (indicative) Fully fixed rate: Starting from 5.99% up to 7.29% on reducing
balance per annum. The rate will remain unchanged for the
loan period.
Annualised Percentage Rate Starting from 6.12% up to 7.43 % per annum. APR is a reference
(APR) (indicative) rate, which includes applicable interest, fees, and charges of the
product, expressed as an annualised rate.
Loan Repayment Your loan repayment is on Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) basis
and is fixed for the entire loan period for fixed rate and may vary
based on EIBOR for variable rate. EMI is calculated by adding the
principal loan amount and total interest and dividing this total by
the loan period in months. This is done using a reducing balance
method which means that as your loan balance reduces, with every
Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) payment, the interest portion of
your EMI will reduce.
Key Fees & Charges
Loan Processing Fee 1.05% of the loan amount, inclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT) Min
amount of AED 2,500 plus VAT. This is a non-refundable fee, and
will not be returned, in case you decide to withdraw the loan
application
Early Settlement/ Foreclosure 1.05% of Principal Outstanding amount (maximum AED 10,500)
Fee Partial Settlement Fee
Partial Settlement Fee 1.05% of payment made (maximum AED 10,500)
Late Payment Fee AED 262.50 (inclusive of VAT), if the monthly repayment is not
received in full, by the due date

Additional Information
• The loan amount you are eligible for and the interest rate offered may differ from the representative example as
shown above. This will depend on your credit profile and the affordability assessment carried out by Emirates
NBD Bank (P.J.S.C.) (the “Bank”).
• The processing of your application leading to the disbursal of the loan may take up to 10 business days from the
time the Bank receives complete and accurate information and documentation. In case your loan is being taken
over from another institution, the processing time may take longer depending on when all documentation is
received from the other institution.
• All interest charges applicable are calculated from the date of loan disbursal. This will also apply in the case of a
deferred First Payment Date.
• The Bank reserves the right to amend the General Terms and Conditions for Business Banking Products (including
any relevant applications and documents) by notice to you in accordance with applicable law.
• In case you fail to fulfil your obligations as per the terms and conditions applicable for the loan, before and during
your relationship with us, there will be consequences including, but not limited to penalties.
• Your Fixed Deposit will be lien marked as security in favor of the Bank, until the loan is repaid in full.
• We do not charge interest on accrued interest.
• You can contact the Bank for any enquiries, assistance or complaints at any of its branches or by visiting its
website https://www.emiratesnbd.com/en/customer-care/complaints/.
• The cooling-off period allows you to cancel the product within five (5) business days of signing the application or
offer letter and the Bank will not be able to proceed until this period expires, unless you waive this right. To cancel
within this period you must give the Bank notice.
